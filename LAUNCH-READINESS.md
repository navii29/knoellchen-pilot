# Knöllchen-Pilot — Launch-Readiness Audit

_Generated by a 16-agent parallel audit (14 dimensions → completeness critic → synthesis). Each finding cites file:line evidence._

## Verdict

NO-GO for a self-serve paid public launch today. The single biggest reason: there is no way to charge SaaS customers at all (no payment provider, no plan/trial state, no gating), so the advertised 49/149€ plans and 14-day trial cannot collect a euro or enforce a limit. On top of that, the money/legal core that DOES exist produces legally-invalid invoices (no sequential Rechnungsnummer, no IBAN/bank details, no Kleinunternehmer path) and can double-bill renters, while the published privacy policy describes a different processing reality than the deployed code. It can run only as a manual-invoice, contracted design-partner pilot — not a public paid launch.


## 🔴 Launch blockers (12)


### 1. No SaaS billing system exists — cannot charge the advertised plans, and no plan/trial state is persisted — _2-4 weeks_

- **Why:** Marketing sells Starter 49€/Pro 149€/Enterprise + a 14-day trial, but there is no Stripe/Paddle/etc package, no checkout/subscription/webhook route, and no payment keys. The organizations table has no plan/tier/trial_ends_at/subscription_status column across the base schema and all 30 migrations, and middleware only checks auth — never plan/trial. Every signup is therefore a permanent free account, the '20 vehicles' Starter cap and Pro-only features are unenforced, and revenue collection is 100% manual (FAQ admits 'Pläne werden manuell aktiviert'). You can onboard but cannot bill, gate, or expire anyone.
- **Fix:** Integrate a payment provider (Stripe Checkout + Customer Portal + subscription webhooks). Add plan/trial/subscription columns to organizations, wire them in bootstrap, enforce trial expiry + plan quotas in middleware/layout, and add subscription invoicing/management. Until then, launch only as a manual-invoice contracted pilot, not self-serve.
- **Evidence:** package.json has no payment dep; no billing route under src/app/api; .env.local.example has no payment keys; no plan/tier/trial column in any migration; src/middleware.ts only redirects unauthenticated users; AppleFAQ.tsx:23-24

### 2. Invoices are legally invalid: non-sequential/non-unique Rechnungsnummer and NO bank account to pay into — _3-5 days_

- **Why:** The Rechnungsnummer is ticket_nr = `KP-${Date.now().toString().slice(-6)}` (src/lib/utils.ts:49) — not sequential, repeats every ~16.6 min, no per-org scoping or DB uniqueness — a direct §14 Abs.4 Nr.4 UStG violation on both the renter Rechnung and the partner-Provisionsrechnung. Worse, the invoice demands payment 'innerhalb von 14 Tagen' but shows no IBAN/BIC: there is NO bank field in the Organization type/schema/any migration, and the PDF footer is the hardcoded placeholder 'Bitte Bankdaten in den Einstellungen ergänzen' (pdf-generator.ts:250). The product's entire job is producing payable, lawful invoices, and it does neither.
- **Fix:** Add a per-org atomic invoice-counter (table + DB function), separate invoice_nr from ticket_nr, and backfill. Add IBAN/BIC/Kontoinhaber fields to org schema + settings + invoice footer. Block invoice generation until bank details are set.
- **Evidence:** src/lib/utils.ts:49; pdf-generator.ts:410,420 (nr) and :250 (placeholder); no IBAN in types.ts/supabase-schema.sql/migrations; partner-invoice-pdf.ts:60 same defect

### 3. VAT hardcoded at 19% — no Kleinunternehmer (§19 UStG) path forces a common customer class to issue illegal invoices — _2-3 days_

- **Why:** VAT_RATE = 0.19 is hardcoded in src/lib/charge.ts:3 and the PDF always prints the 19% MwSt note. Many small Autovermietungen are Kleinunternehmer and must NOT show/charge VAT (and must print the §19 note); for them this product generates invoices with wrongly-shown VAT they then owe per §14c UStG. No per-org kleinunternehmer flag exists.
- **Fix:** Add a kleinunternehmer flag on organizations; make VAT rate per-org; suppress VAT lines and print the §19 UStG note when set.
- **Evidence:** src/lib/charge.ts:3 (VAT_RATE=0.19, no override); pdf-generator.ts:494 always prints 19% note; no kleinunternehmer flag in types.ts/migrations

### 4. Same fine can be billed and dunned twice — no idempotency on inbound email or upload — _1-2 days_

- **Why:** The inbound webhook inserts a ticket with inbound_email_id = payload.MessageID (postmark-inbound/route.ts:156) but never SELECTs for an existing row with that MessageID first, and migration 002 makes the column a plain non-unique TEXT. Postmark inbound is at-least-once, so a redelivered webhook creates a duplicate ticket; a notice arriving by both upload and email is also undeduped. Result: two invoices with different §14 numbers and two dunning tracks for one fine — a renter billed twice. Direct money-correctness and German invoicing-law exposure on the core revenue path.
- **Fix:** SELECT by inbound_email_id before insert + add a UNIQUE constraint; add a soft dedup heuristic on reference_nr/plate/offense_date for the upload-vs-email case.
- **Evidence:** src/app/api/webhook/postmark-inbound/route.ts:156,162 (insert, no prior existence check); migration 002_email_automation.sql (non-unique index)

### 5. Original Bußgeldbescheid scan can be silently overwritten in storage (evidence destruction) — _1 day (folds into the invoice-number fix)_

- **Why:** ticket_nr is the storage path prefix for the original scan in BOTH manual upload (tickets/route.ts:31) and inbound (postmark-inbound/route.ts:132), both with upsert:true. Because the 6-digit timestamp value collides every ~16.6 min and is not per-org unique, two tickets can resolve to the same `${orgId}/${ticketNr}/upload.ext` and the second upload overwrites the first ticket's source evidence. For a product whose value is producing legally-defensible documents, silently destroying the proof of an earlier case is a data-integrity/legal-discovery blocker.
- **Fix:** Use a UUID or DB-sequence-backed unique id for both the storage path and the invoice number; never upsert onto a colliding prefix.
- **Evidence:** src/lib/utils.ts:49; src/app/api/tickets/route.ts:31-36 (upsert:true); src/app/api/webhook/postmark-inbound/route.ts:132-136 (upsert:true)

### 6. GDPR right-to-erasure is broken and incomplete for ID/license data — _1-2 weeks_

- **Why:** Deleting a customer only deletes the DB row (customers/[id]/route.ts:95) and never calls storage.remove() for license_photo_path/id_card_photo_path — highly sensitive ID-document images are orphaned in storage forever after an 'erasure'. Worse, contracts.customer_id REFERENCES customers(id) was added with NO ON DELETE clause (003_customers.sql:65), so Postgres RESTRICT blocks deletion of ANY contracted renter outright. Plus there is no org/account self-deletion (org/route.ts has only GET/PATCH) and no data export (Art.20). For an EU product storing ID cards and signatures, erasure is both legally required and currently non-functional for the common case.
- **Fix:** On customer/contract delete, remove all storage objects (license/id-card/contract PDFs/photos). Add ON DELETE CASCADE/SET NULL with dependent cleanup. Add an org-deletion endpoint that wipes the tenant, and a JSON/ZIP export route.
- **Evidence:** src/app/api/customers/[id]/route.ts:42-43,95 (no storage.remove); supabase/migrations/003_customers.sql:65 (no ON DELETE); src/app/api/org/route.ts (no DELETE)

### 7. Published Datenschutzerklärung describes a different processing reality than the code (wrong/missing sub-processors) — _0.5 day_

- **Why:** The policy names 'Mailgun der Sinch Email' as the email processor (datenschutz/page.tsx:239-260), but all email actually flows through Postmark (src/lib/postmark.ts) — Mailgun appears nowhere in code. Shopify (receives renter name/email/address) and lexoffice (receives renter full name + home address, lexoffice.ts:50-60) are live sub-processors that are NOT disclosed at all. An inaccurate/incomplete Art.13/30 processor list for a product handling licence+ID data is concrete DSGVO non-compliance and Abmahnung/authority exposure.
- **Fix:** Correct the email provider to Postmark; add Postmark/Shopify/lexoffice with legal entity, location, third-country basis and AVV status. Also fix the FAQ 'Mailgun' string.
- **Evidence:** src/app/datenschutz/page.tsx:239-260 (Mailgun/Sinch); src/lib/postmark.ts; Shopify/lexoffice absent from datenschutz; src/lib/lexoffice.ts:50-60

### 8. All outbound email is silently redirected to a personal Gmail, and unset sender falls back to the vendor's own domain — _0.5 day (+1 day for verified-sender gating)_

- **Why:** EMAIL_TEST_OVERRIDE=edlmairfridolin@gmail.com is set in live .env.local: every renter re-bill, authority Fahrerermittlung, magic link and portal invite is redirected to that inbox and never reaches the real recipient, while the UI marks letter_sent=true. Separately, when an org has not configured sender_email, send/route.ts:80 sends From: `${org.name} <noreply@knoellchen-pilot.de>` (the VENDOR's domain) on behalf of an arbitrary rental company — an impersonation/deliverability problem and a silent failure of the advertised 'send from your own domain'.
- **Fix:** Unset EMAIL_TEST_OVERRIDE for prod and add a guard so production refuses to boot/send with it set. Block sending (or require a verified sender) instead of falling back to the vendor domain.
- **Evidence:** .env.local: EMAIL_TEST_OVERRIDE=edlmairfridolin@gmail.com; src/app/api/tickets/[id]/send/route.ts:76-80,112,117

### 9. GPS-Tracking ships fabricated coordinates into operational decisions with no in-product warning — _0.5 day (flag off) / 1-2 weeks (real API)_

- **Why:** src/lib/echoes.ts is an explicit stub ('KEINE offizielle Echoes-API-Doku… realistische Dummy-Daten'); getDevicePosition returns deterministic random coords around Munich for ECHO-DEMO devices. The vehicle GPS route writes these into vehicles.last_gps_lat/lng and GpsLocation.tsx renders them on a real OSM map at 6-decimal precision with a timestamp and ZERO stub warning (the only warning is on the settings connection-test). GPS is advertised; an operator could dispatch a repo/recovery agent to invented coordinates. A banner is not enough.
- **Fix:** Hard-disable / feature-flag the GPS feature off in production until a real Echoes API exists; do not write or render fabricated coordinates.
- **Evidence:** src/lib/echoes.ts:4-5,72,105-119; src/app/api/vehicles/[id]/gps/route.ts:70-77; src/components/vehicle/GpsLocation.tsx (no warning)

### 10. No dashboard password reset and no email verification for the paying customer's account — _2-3 days_

- **Why:** There is zero resetPasswordForEmail/updateUser, no /forgot or /reset page, and no /auth/callback in src — a rental-company owner who forgets their password is permanently locked out of the data and documents they pay for. Registration also force-signs-in after signUp to bypass any email-confirmation gate, so anyone can register an org under any (unverified) email, and the org sender/inbound identity derives from it. Table-stakes for a paid B2B SaaS, both completely absent.
- **Fix:** Add Supabase password reset (resetPasswordForEmail + /auth/callback + reset page) and enable email verification (stop force-sign-in, add code-exchange callback).
- **Evidence:** grep for resetPasswordForEmail/updateUser/forgot/reset across src returns nothing; src/app/(auth)/register/page.tsx:26-44 force-signs-in

### 11. No EU data residency pinning — renter ID cards/licenses processed on US infra by default — _1-2 days_

- **Why:** vercel.json has no regions key and nothing pins functions to fra1/eu-central, so Vercel Serverless Functions default to US East (iad1). Every Vision call on driver-license + ID-card images, every PDF generation, and the inbound webhook (raw Bußgeldbescheid + PII) executes on US infrastructure for a product that explicitly handles special-category-adjacent ID data — a concrete DSGVO transfer exposure without a documented basis.
- **Fix:** Set vercel.json regions:["fra1"], confirm the Supabase project is in an EU region, and write the data-flow/DPA note for B2B customers.
- **Evidence:** vercel.json (no regions key); no fra1/eu-central anywhere in repo config

### 12. Renter portal (ID-card/license uploads) is search-engine indexable — no robots.txt / noindex — _0.5 day_

- **Why:** There is no public/robots.txt, no src/app/robots.ts, and no noindex meta/X-Robots-Tag on /portal or /dashboard. The customer portal collects driver licenses, ID cards, photos and signatures and is crawlable/indexable by default. For a paid German product handling ID documents this is a DSGVO Art.32/data-minimization exposure, not an SEO nicety.
- **Fix:** Ship a robots route disallowing /portal,/dashboard,/onboarding,/api and add X-Robots-Tag: noindex headers on those segments.
- **Evidence:** No public/robots.txt, no src/app/robots.ts; grep for noindex/X-Robots across src/app/portal, src/app/dashboard, middleware returns nothing

## 🟡 Important before launch (10)


### 1. No error monitoring, no alerting, and silent failure of the automated inbound-parse path — _3-5 days_

- **Why:** Zero Sentry/Highlight/OTel integration and no instrumentation.ts — server exceptions in prod are invisible. The inbound-email Vision parse+match is best-effort inside a try/catch whose only handling is console.error; on failure the ticket sits in status 'neu' (indistinguishable from a fresh upload) with no parse_error state, no failed ticket_logs action, and no alert. A Bußgeldbescheid that fails to parse can blow its statutory Einspruchsfrist with no human noticing — the rental company's direct legal liability. No deadline-driven worklist surfaces tickets by due date anywhere.
- **Fix:** Wire Sentry + instrumentation.ts; add a parse_error/'auslese_fehler' ticket state with an operator-visible queue + alert; render a deadline-sorted worklist in the dashboard.

### 2. No automated tests and no CI on the money/legal paths — _2-3 days for first suite + CI_

- **Why:** No test framework or test files at all, and no .github/workflows. charge.ts (VAT/euro amounts), plate.ts (decides who gets legally accused, with documented recurring mis-parse history), and the match route are completely unverified; a rounding or normalization regression ships wrong amounts or accuses the wrong renter to a German authority. A plate-normalization fix was committed as recently as Jun 15 — exactly what a test suite would pin.
- **Fix:** Add Vitest with a money+plate+match suite first; add a GitHub Actions workflow running lint + tsc --noEmit + next build + vitest on every PR.

### 3. Inbound webhook is fail-open and AI endpoints are unthrottled — _1-2 days_

- **Why:** The Postmark inbound webhook only checks a token IF POSTMARK_WEBHOOK_SECRET is set (it is blank in the example), so unset = fully open: anyone who learns an org slug can inject forged tickets, burn Anthropic spend, and trigger a Fahrerermittlung containing real renter PII. No Postmark signature verification. Separately, no rate limiting on authenticated AI endpoints (parse, assistant, portal document parsing) — a single trial account can run up unbounded Vision cost.
- **Fix:** Make the webhook secret mandatory in prod (fail-closed) and verify Postmark signature/Basic-Auth; add per-org rate limiting/usage caps on AI endpoints.

### 4. Email 'sent' not verified against Postmark ErrorCode; no bounce/delivery handling — _1-2 days_

- **Why:** sendEmail returns the raw Postmark response and the send route treats any non-throwing response as success, setting letter_sent/authority_sent=true. Postmark can return 200 with a non-zero ErrorCode (suppressed/inactive recipient, hard bounce). A ticket can be marked 'weiterbelastet' while the official letter never delivered, with no bounce/delivery webhook anywhere — a silent failure on the legal/billing path.
- **Fix:** Check ErrorCode before marking sent; add a Postmark delivery/bounce webhook and persist delivery state.

### 5. Advertised 'Mahnstufen-Tracking' dunning does not exist — _1-2 weeks (build) or 0.5 day (remove claim)_

- **Why:** Pricing/marketing sells automatic dunning, but reminder_level is never written or read in src/app/api or src/lib, there is no Mahnung template, no overdue detection, and no cron (only /api/health). The reports page even says 'Mahnstufen folgen in Kürze'. It is an advertised-but-fake paid feature.
- **Fix:** Either build reminder escalation (overdue cron + Mahnung PDF/email + reminder_level writes) or remove the claim from marketing until it exists.

### 6. No multi-user/team support despite a B2B 'users.role' model — _1 week_

- **Why:** There is no invite endpoint or settings UI to add colleagues, and the RLS policy 'Users see self' (id = auth.uid()) structurally prevents an org from ever having a second effective user. A rental company with multiple staff must share one login — a security and usability gap for a B2B product.
- **Fix:** Add an invite flow + member management UI and fix the users RLS policy to be org-scoped so members can coexist.

### 7. WCAG AA contrast failure of secondary text across the dashboard (BITV 2.0/EN 301 549) — _2-3 days_

- **Why:** text-ink-muted (#86868b), used 586x for meta/secondary text, measures 2.65-3.6:1 on the new Liquid Glass surfaces — below the 4.5:1 AA threshold. For a paid product sold to German B2B/public-sector buyers this is real accessibility-law exposure. Modals also lack role=dialog/focus traps and several search inputs lack accessible names.
- **Fix:** Darken ink.muted to ~4.6:1 on white and re-verify over the aurora; add dialog ARIA + focus traps to modals and aria-labels to search inputs.

### 8. Legal links absent inside the app/portal; no portal data-processing notice or AVV document — _1-2 days (+legal template)_

- **Why:** §5 DDG and Art.13 require the Impressum/privacy notice to be reachable where data is collected, but no /impressum, /datenschutz or /agb link exists under /dashboard or /portal, and the portal (which collects ID cards) presents no Art.13 notice at the point of collection. No executable AVV/DPA template ships despite the AGB promising one.
- **Fix:** Add legal links to the dashboard and portal footers, present a data-processing notice + /datenschutz link in the portal check-in/sign flow, and ship a signable AVV.

### 9. Deployment hygiene: missing env keys, no region/Node pin, no migration runbook — _1 day_

- **Why:** PORTAL_JWT_SECRET (load-bearing — portal throws without it) and CRON_SECRET/INTERNAL_API_SECRET/PORTAL_COOKIE_DOMAIN/ECHOES_BASE_URL are absent from .env.local.example, so a deploy following it produces a broken /portal. Node version is unpinned (no engines/.nvmrc), there is no documented migration apply order (README is create-next-app boilerplate, base schema lives outside supabase/migrations/), and no backup/PITR posture is documented.
- **Fix:** Sync .env.local.example, pin Node via engines/.nvmrc, document the migration apply order + enable Supabase PITR + write a deploy runbook.

### 10. Marketing-site launch polish: no 404, error boundary, OpenGraph, sitemap, metadataBase — _2-3 days_

- **Why:** No not-found.tsx/error.tsx anywhere (bad links and render errors show Next.js default screens), no OG/Twitter card or OG image (B2B links on LinkedIn/WhatsApp/Slack render as a bare URL), no sitemap, and no metadataBase. Collectively this makes a paid public launch read as unfinished.
- **Fix:** Add branded not-found.tsx + global-error.tsx, OpenGraph metadata + OG image, sitemap.ts, and metadataBase.

## ✅ Already solid

- Multi-tenant authorization is genuinely strong and consistent: every admin-client [id] route scopes by org_id (verified across ~20 routes), the two prior IDORs (parse, match) are fixed with no analogous gap, and nested sub-resources scope by both child id and org_id. RLS is enabled with a matching org-scoped policy on all 19 tables via a safe SECURITY DEFINER current_org_id() helper.
- The customer portal (renter) custom-JWT auth is production-grade: bcrypt cost 12, HS256 JWT with a runtime secret-length guard, httpOnly+secure+sameSite cookies, a shared guard enforcing org_id AND customer_id on every portal route, enumeration-safe generic errors, rate-limited login/magic-link, and single-use 24h magic tokens that also serve as renter password recovery.
- The core Strafzettel pipeline is real and well-built, not vaporware: Claude Vision parse, normalizePlate-based driver matching with org isolation, jsPDF DIN 5008 Form B documents, Postmark send with attachments + ticket_logs audit trail, and a real inbound-email-to-ticket pipeline (honestly labeled Beta).
- German legal pages are written by someone competent — real entity (Southern Phoenix GmbH), real HRB/USt-IdNr, correct BayLDA supervisory authority, B2B-only AGB with a product-specific AI-output disclaimer, and a defensible §25 TDDDG cookie position. No placeholder/lorem text; this clears the 'fake legal copy = instant Abmahnung' bar (the remaining issue is sub-processor accuracy, not boilerplate).
- SSRF defenses on org integrations are solid (Shopify domain regex + https + redirect:'error', CDN allow-list), org API credentials are stored per-org and stripped from client responses, and all Anthropic/Postmark/lexoffice calls are server-only.
- Database migrations are clean: sequentially numbered 001-030 with no gaps, idempotent, non-destructive (zero DROP/TRUNCATE/unscoped DELETE), with private storage buckets and indexes covering the documented hot paths.
- Email templates and the EMAIL_TEST_OVERRIDE safety mechanism are carefully built (HTML-escaped fields, plaintext alternatives, test-mode logging of intended vs actual recipient), and the health/keep-alive endpoint is genuinely well-designed (real DB probe, 503 on failure, CRON_SECRET-aware, prevents Supabase auto-pause).
- Money logic is isolated into small pure functions (charge.ts, plate.ts, km.ts, margin.ts, pricing.ts) — good architecture that makes the missing tests cheap to add. VAT arithmetic on the fee itself (net→19%→gross, round2) is computed correctly, and fine vs fee are cleanly separated. next.config.mjs does not suppress TS/lint errors and tsconfig is strict.
- Accessibility fundamentals are good: pinch-zoom enabled, real <label>s (not placeholder-as-label), correct alt/aria-hidden on images, keyboard-operable FileDrop, real buttons everywhere, a polished mobile sidebar drawer with ESC/scroll-lock/focus handling, and prefers-reduced-motion respected on the aurora/reveal animations.

## Recommended sequence

1. DECIDE THE LAUNCH SHAPE FIRST: a self-serve paid launch needs the full billing build (2-4wk); a contracted design-partner pilot with manual invoicing can skip billing but must still clear the legal/correctness blockers below. Recommend the pilot path to get to real customers fast.
2. Quick legal/safety stops (1-2 days total): unset EMAIL_TEST_OVERRIDE + add a prod guard; fix the Datenschutzerklärung processor list (Postmark/Shopify/lexoffice, drop Mailgun); add robots.ts + noindex on /portal,/dashboard; pin Vercel to fra1 + confirm Supabase EU; make the inbound webhook secret mandatory; sync .env.local.example (PORTAL_JWT_SECRET etc.).
3. Invoice legality (4-6 days): per-org sequential invoice number + UUID storage paths (also fixes the evidence-overwrite blocker), IBAN/bank fields end-to-end, and the Kleinunternehmer/§19 VAT path. Without these the product cannot issue a lawful payable invoice.
4. Money correctness + erasure (1 week): add inbound idempotency (inbound_email_id UNIQUE + dedup heuristic); implement working Art.17 erasure (storage cleanup + FK ON DELETE) and org/account deletion; block the email vendor-domain fallback; feature-flag GPS off in production.
5. Account lifecycle + safety net (1 week): dashboard password reset + email verification; minimal multi-user invite; Sentry + a parse_error state with a deadline-sorted operator worklist; check Postmark ErrorCode; first Vitest money+plate suite + CI gate.
6. Pre-launch polish (2-3 days): 404/error pages, OpenGraph/sitemap/metadataBase, legal links in app + portal data-processing notice, AA contrast fix, AVV document.
7. Only after the above, and (for self-serve) after the full Stripe build with plan/trial enforcement, flip the public paid launch.
